A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Before your next move.

How do I start a match?

Create an account, verify your email, and sign in to the app. In the lobby, create a challenge or accept an open one. The player who accepts moves first. Both players need an internet connection.

What are the three game modes?

Classic XO is a 3 × 3 board: connect three. Bombs uses a 4 × 4 board, connect-four wins and hidden bombs that reset marks. Tactics XO keeps four living marks per player, gives you one capture or shield, and uses pressure scoring at move 40 if nobody connects four. The app explains each mode before play.

Does the website warm-up play against another person?

No. The warm-up on this website is a solo game against a bot. Open QALIM tickTack to challenge real players and save match results.

Where can I download the app?

The home page shows Apple App Store, Google Play, Huawei AppGallery and direct APK channels. A channel marked “Coming soon” is not available yet. Until the links are added, you can play through the web link.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Choose “Forgot password?” on the app’s sign-in page. Open the email link, choose a new password, then return to the app and sign in. Check your spam folder if the email hasn’t arrived.

How do I delete my account or report a problem?

Use the request form below. For deletion, enter the email registered to your account and confirm you understand the consequences. For a safety concern, choose “Safety report” and share only the details needed to investigate.

YOUR DATA. YOUR CHOICES.

Privacy policy.

LAST UPDATED: 9 OCTOBER 2026

This policy covers QALIM tickTack’s game, mobile app and this website. QALIM uses information to create accounts, run live matches, save results and respond to requests. We do not sell your personal information.

Information used by the service

  • Account information: your email address, chosen player name, account identifier and authentication information. Passwords are handled by Supabase Authentication; we do not receive your password through this website’s forms.
  • Game information: challenges, participants, moves, final boards, results, game modes and match timestamps. Your player name and the matches you share with an opponent are visible to that opponent; open challenges display player names in the lobby.
  • Notifications: if you allow push notifications, the app registers a device push token associated with your account to send challenge alerts. You can disable notifications in your device settings.
  • Technical information: the services hosting the app may process IP addresses, browser or device information and operational logs needed for reliability and security.
  • Requests: the email address and optional player name or message you enter when asking for deletion, privacy help or reporting a safety concern.

Why we use it and who processes it

We use this information for sign-in and verification, live gameplay, match history, optional alerts, service security and handling your requests. Supabase provides authentication and database services; Vercel hosts the web service; Expo and Firebase support mobile push delivery. Our configured email provider processes form submissions. These providers process the data necessary to deliver their services and may operate in countries outside your own.

Device storage and tracking

The app stores session information so you can stay signed in, along with preferences such as sound settings. Essential storage supports the game and authentication. This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. It does not request camera, microphone or location access.

Retention and deletion

Account and game information is kept while the account is active. You can request account deletion using the form below. After verifying ownership, we aim to remove your account and associated live-service data within 30 days. Match records involving your account will be removed or de-identified, which can also affect your opponent’s shared history. Limited records may be retained where required for a legal obligation or to investigate abuse; we will explain any applicable exception. Provider backups expire according to the provider’s retention schedule and are not used to reactivate a deleted account.

Request correspondence is kept only as needed to process and document the request or meet an applicable legal obligation. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service can promise absolute security.

Your choices and children’s privacy

You can change notification permissions, sign out, request access or correction, and request deletion. Use the form below for privacy questions or to exercise these choices; availability of statutory rights depends on your location. A parent or guardian can use the same form to raise a concern about a child’s account. If an account was created without consent required by applicable law, we will investigate and remove it as appropriate.

For privacy enquiries, send a privacy request. The form delivers to the QALIM support team; you do not need an active session to request deletion.

KEEP THE RIVALRY ON THE BOARD

Safety & community standards.

LAST UPDATED: 9 OCTOBER 2026

QALIM tickTack is a two-player game. Competitive play should be enjoyable and respectful. The app does not provide chat, photo or video uploads. These standards apply to player names and any interaction with our service or support team.

Play fair. Treat people well.

Do not use abusive, threatening, discriminatory or sexually explicit player names. Do not impersonate others, exploit the game, automate unfair play, harass other players or attempt to access another account. Keep personal information out of player names.

Zero tolerance for child exploitation

Child sexual abuse and exploitation, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexualisation of children and attempts to facilitate such abuse, are strictly prohibited. We investigate reports related to QALIM and may restrict or remove accounts. Where required, we report suspected illegal activity to the appropriate authorities and cooperate with lawful requests.

Raise a concern safely

Use the safety-report option in the form below to identify the player name, approximate time or match reference and describe your concern. Do not send explicit images, videos, CSAM or unnecessary personal information. We review reports, may ask for further information and take action appropriate to the evidence. The form is not monitored as an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate child-protection authority.

Account action and review

Responses can include a request to change a player name, temporary restrictions or account removal. If you believe an action was mistaken, send a privacy or safety request so we can review it. Parents and guardians can report concerns about a child’s use of the service through the same route.
